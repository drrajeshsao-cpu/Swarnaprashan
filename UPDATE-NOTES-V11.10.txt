MAHAMAYA SWARNAPRASHAN V11.10 — STAFF AUDIT TRAIL

Added independent accountability fields:
- Child Registered By
- Swarnaprashan Administered By
- Payment Received / Status Known By

Registry now displays all three in a Staff Audit column.
Clinical Treatment step records Administered By independently of billing.
Dashboard staff table shows monthly: Children Registered, Doses Administered, Payment Entries, Total Collected, Cash, UPI, Pending, Credit/Udhari.
Child details show registration and latest administration/payment responsibility.
Search also matches staff names.
Old records without historical attribution display Not recorded (legacy); the app does not guess.
New child registrations default Registered By to the currently signed-in user but remain selectable.
Existing Firebase authentication/login logic was not redesigned.
