MAHAMAYA SWARNAPRASHAN V11.6 — PAYMENT & COLLECTION LEDGER

Only requested operational/accounting additions were made.

Added:
- Separate payment transaction ledger per child.
- Payment statuses: Paid, Part Paid, Pending, Credit/Udhari, Pay Later,
  Home Use - Already Paid, Complimentary/Free, Waived/No Charge.
- Methods: Cash, UPI, Card, Bank Transfer, Credit/Udhari, Pay Later,
  Home Use Prepaid, Complimentary/Free, Other.
- Amount billed, amount received, automatic balance due.
- Receipt/UPI/reference and payment notes.
- Payment recorded-by user name.
- Child Registry payment column and payment-status filter.
- Child detail payment ledger and Record Payment button.
- Dashboard automatic monthly collection totals: cash, UPI, total collected,
  total outstanding, due-child count, transaction count.
- Payment ledger syncs as a dedicated Firebase collection for multi-user clinic use.
- Child age remains visible with name and ID.

Authentication policy:
- Firebase authentication/login logic was not redesigned.
- Existing stable role and persistence behavior retained.
