MAHAMAYA SWARNAPRASHAN V11.8 — STAFF FINANCE + INVENTORY

STAFF-WISE SALES / COLLECTION
- Payment entry now includes Sale / Dose Handled By.
- Default choices: Dr Rajesh Sao, Dr Ravi Chandrakar, Miss Mansi, Other.
- Staff list can be edited from Settings as comma-separated names.
- Dashboard shows staff-wise monthly Collected, Cash, UPI, Pending and Credit/Udhari.

INVENTORY / PROCUREMENT
- New Inventory & Stock module.
- Swarnabrahma Yog Tablet:
  * Tablet
  * Strip = 30 tablets
  * Box = 5 strips = 150 tablets
  * MRP, purchase price, total purchase cost, vendor, payment method, batch/brand
  * Purchase, clinic use, home-use/sale, self use, family/relative free, wastage/damage, adjustments
- Cow Ghee and Honey:
  * g, ml, kg, L, pack
  * exact pack size can be entered in Brand/Batch field
  * purchase cost/vendor/payment method/use history
- Feeding Spoon:
  * piece/pack purchase and usage
- Other consumables supported.
- Dashboard shows current tablet/ghee/honey/spoon stock.
- Inventory ledger is cloud-synced for doctor/staff continuity.
- Existing child Name + ID + Age display retained.
- Firebase authentication/login code was not redesigned.
