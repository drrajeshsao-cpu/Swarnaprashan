MAHAMAYA SWARNAPRASHAN V11.7 — CONFIGURABLE BILLING DASHBOARD

Default billing:
- Swarnaprashan default rate = ₹250 per dose.
- Configurable from Settings > Swarnaprashan Rate / Dose.
- New payment entry defaults to quantity 1 × current rate.
- Total amount auto-calculates from Quantity × Rate.
- Paid in Full automatically fills received amount equal to bill.
- Home-use multiple doses can be billed by quantity × same configurable rate.

Dashboard finance intelligence:
- Expected billing this month
- Total collection this month
- Cash collection
- UPI collection
- Card + Bank collection
- Pending balance
- Credit/Udhari outstanding
- Total outstanding
- Children with due
- Home-use prepaid
- All payment ledger entries

Per-child:
- billed, paid, due
- method
- status
- quantity × rate
- transaction history
- child name + ID + age retained

Authentication/login design not changed.
